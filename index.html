Kindly skip

Not "bad" colours — just ones that fight your softness and drain your face. If a piece is one of these, put it back (or keep it far from your face — a handbag or shoe is fine).

Using this in the store

Hold it to your jaw in natural light near a window. Your right colour makes skin look clear and lifted; a wrong one casts grey or makes blemishes pop.

Match the mood, not just the hex. Everything here is muted and warm. A colour that looks "dusty," "toasted," or "sun-faded" is usually yours; anything "bright," "icy," or "jewel" usually isn't.

Build outfits from your neutrals — camel, cocoa, oat, soft olive — then add one spice or berry as the star. Skip black and stark white; reach for espresso and warm ivory instead.

For makeup, undertone beats shade name. Test foundation on your jaw in daylight and pick the one that disappears. Warm/golden or neutral — never pink or grey.
